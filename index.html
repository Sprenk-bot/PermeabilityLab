    What the graph means

    Horizontal axis: elapsed simulated time in seconds, from 0 to 120. Vertical axis: cumulative water collected in millilitres. Every material uses the same linear axes and evenly spaced tick values.

    At the same time, a higher line means more water has been collected. A steeper line means a faster flow rate.

    Dotted line: model estimate from the selected time and current settings. Solid line: recorded result, which keeps its earlier values when settings change. Both are simulated, not field measurements.

    When materials differ greatly, a low-flow line may sit close to zero on the shared scale. Read the labelled mL values at the same time to compare them.

    THE BIG IDEA

    More pores doesn’t always mean faster flow.

    ✳

    Soil contains spaces between particles. Those spaces store water, but the size and connection of the spaces affect how quickly water can move. A clay can have lots of pore space and still let water through slowly because its pores are tiny and poorly connected.

    ◌Porosity How much open space is in the material.
    ≠
    ↗Permeability How easily connected spaces let water through.

    Where to check the science

    These sources support the science and Perth setting. No unverified conductivity range is presented as measured data here; advanced values are clearly marked as illustrative model inputs.

    • USGS — Porosity and permeabilityExplains pore space and how connected openings allow groundwater to move.
